Medieval Windmill Scene - Blender to Unreal Engine 5

This is a demo scene in Unreal Engine 5, showcasing my first complex animated 3D model. I created the model in Blender, guided by 3DTudor's 'Blender to Unreal Engine 5' Udemy Course. My goal was to learn more about Blender, 3D Modelling and UE5 enviroment design. Sounds in the video were designed to fit each aspect of the scene, then they were implemented using MetaSounds. Other UE5 assets used in the scene include:

  • Wheat | CropCraft Studios - Ultimate Farming
  • Landscape Material | MAWI United - MW Landscape Auto Material
  • Background Trees & Foliage | STF3d - Landscape Pro 2.0 Auto-Generated Material
  • River | UE5 Water Plugin
